Grid Edge Gateway

A site controller in Rust that sits between a German grid operator and a commercial prosumer site. It speaks IEC 60870-5-104 to the operator's control centre and SunSpec Modbus to the inverters, chargers and heat pump, and in between it enforces §14a EnWG dimming and feed-in limits. What runs below is that code, compiled to WebAssembly.

How it works

Towards the DSO

The gateway is an IEC 60870-5-104 controlled station, the protocol German DSOs require for telecontrol of plants from 100 kW. Commands are confirmed (or refused with a negative confirmation), measurements go out spontaneously with time tags, and the link runs over TLS with client certificates in the spirit of IEC 62351-3. The protocol stack is written from scratch and tested against lib60870.

The control rules

During §14a dimming the controllable devices may draw at least Pmin,14a from the grid; the EMS decides how to split it. Here the heat pump keeps 40% of its rating, as many cars as fit get the 6 A minimum, least-charged first, and PV surplus is added on top. After the signal ends, power returns over 5 minutes instead of in one step.

Towards the devices

Inverters and meter are found by walking the SunSpec model chain. Inverter limits are written without a revert timeout, so they stay in force if the gateway dies; chargers get a failsafe current of 6 A, which alone is below 4.2 kW. DSO commands are persisted, so a dimming survives a restart.
